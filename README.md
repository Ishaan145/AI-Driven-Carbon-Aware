# Digital Twin–Based Carbon Forecasting and Risk Aware Renewable Energy Optimization for Institutional Campuses

## Theme: AI-Enabled Waste-to-Energy Systems for Decarbonized and Intelligent Infrastructure

## ABSTRACT

Institutions in India consume massive amounts of electricity yet lack real-time visibility into their carbon footprint. Despite having 
renewable infrastructure, campuses rely heavily on coal-based grid power during peak hours, wasting up to 30% of solar generation 
due to poor demand-supply matching. This research presents a Digital Twin-based carbon forecasting system that creates a 
virtual replica of campus energy systems to simulate, predict, and optimize carbon-aware load scheduling. Using machine learning 
models for demand prediction, grid carbon intensity forecasting, and quantile regression for uncertainty quantification, the system 
achieves 63% reduction in carbon emissions while maintaining operational efficiency. The framework integrates IoT sensors, 
cloud computing, and multi-objective optimization to enable real-time decision-making for sustainable energy management.
